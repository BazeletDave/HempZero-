## Overview

This repository documents the research, development, and experimental results underpinning **Rapid Repeats Technology (RRT)** — an innovative plant biotechnology platform developed by David Grand and collaborators at the Grand Research Institute. The RRT framework enables the creation of turmeric (Curcuma longa) and proprietary Panakiea hybrids with **unprecedented perpetual regenerative capacity**, challenging classical plant dormancy biology.

Through advanced hybridization, CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, and computational bioinformatics, this work produces plants that:

- Continuously regenerate without the traditional dormancy cycles  
- Retain full phytochemical profiles essential for medicinal and agricultural applications  
- Support year-round sustainable production and harvesting  

This README summarizes the science, methodologies, and implications of this breakthrough, along with intellectual property considerations and potential collaboration opportunities.

## Scientific Background

### Panakiea Hybrid Development  
Panakiea is a proprietary hybrid derived from turmeric and related Curcuma species optimized for Florida’s climate and soil. Certified by Florida A&M University, it forms the foundation of the RRT program.

### Turmeric Dormancy and Propagation Challenges  
Traditional turmeric plants undergo annual dormancy, limiting production cycles. Overcoming these genetic and hormonal dormancy controls was essential to developing perpetual growth lines.

### CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing & Computational Modeling  
Targeted knockout of genes regulating dormancy and growth suppression pathways was achieved using CRISPR-Cas9 technology. Computational bioinformatics models guided target selection, ensuring maximum regeneration potential without compromising plant health or phytochemical consistency.

## Research Highlights

- **Perpetual Regeneration:** Edited Panakiea and turmeric lines grow continuously year-round without dormancy.  
- **Phytochemical Stability:** Curcuminoid and sesquiterpenoid content remain stable, preserving medicinal qualities.
